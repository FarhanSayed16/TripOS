B2B Travel Platform
Complete Operational Workflow
1. Agent Registration & Login
Create account, verify email/mobile and enter the dedicated agency workspace.
2. Agency Profile Setup
Configure agency name, owner, address, phone, email, website, registration/GST details, logo, brand color,
invoice numbering, bank/payment details, terms, email sender name, reply-to address, signature and
templates.
3. Dashboard & Daily Operations
Show bookings, sales, upcoming trips, pending customer payments, wallet balance, recent quotations and
bookings. Quick actions: Flight, Hotel, Cab, Customer and Quote.
4. Customer CRM
Create/search customers. Store contact details, billing/GST information, traveller details, booking history,
quotations, invoices, payments and notes. Existing customers should auto-fill saved details.
5. Search & Booking
Flights / Hotels / Cabs: Enter requirements fi Search inventory fi Select option fi Revalidate price and
availability fi Select/create customer fi Enter traveller details fi Review price fi Payment fi Supplier
confirmation.
6. Pricing & Markup
Supplier Price + Agent Markup + applicable taxes/fees - discounts = Customer Price. Support fixed and
percentage markup. Keep calculations consistent across quote, payment, booking and invoice.
7. Customized Invoice
Generate an agency-branded PDF containing logo, agency details, invoice number/date, customer details,
booking reference, travel services, itemized charges, taxes, discounts, total, payment status, payment
instructions and terms.
8. Direct Email
From the booking screen: Preview invoice fi Edit recipient/subject/message fi Attach branded PDF fi
Send. Save email history and status. Support resend and failure retry. Configure verified sending domain
with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
9. Booking Management
Booking detail should contain booking ID, supplier reference, customer/traveller details, dates, price/payment
status, supplier confirmation, documents, email history, cancellation/refund status and activity timeline.
10. Roles & Permissions
Agency Owner: full access. Manager: bookings/customers/quotes/team. Travel Agent: bookings/assigned
customers/documents. Finance: payments/invoices/refunds/reconciliation. Platform Admin: platform
configuration/integrations/support.
11. Complete Operational Sequence
First setup: Register fi Verify fi Profile fi Branding fi Invoice Template fi Email Settings. Daily booking:
Search fi Select fi Customer fi Price fi Payment fi Supplier Confirmation. Post-booking: Generate
Invoice fi PDF fi Email fi Save History fi Manage Changes. Finance: Supplier Cost fi Customer Receipt
fi Markup fi Taxes fi Refunds fi Wallet.
12. Development Priority
Phase 1: Authentication, agency profile, dashboard, CRM. Phase 2: Flight/hotel/cab search, booking and
pricing. Phase 3: Invoice builder, PDF and email. Phase 4: Payments, wallet, refunds, reconciliation and
reporting. Phase 5: Permissions, automation, audit logs and AI.
13. Product Principle
Configure once. Book quickly. Manage everything in one place. Communicate professionally. The agent
should manage customers and bookings from one workspace and send professional agency-branded
invoices directly to customers without leaving the platform.
